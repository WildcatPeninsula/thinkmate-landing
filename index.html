🔒 ThinkMate Privacy Policy

What We Collect

ThinkMate does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from children or their parents. All tutoring sessions happen entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

What Happens During Use

  • Your child selects a subject (Math, Reading, Science, Writing, History, or General)
  • The AI tutor works entirely in the browser
  • Session count is stored locally on the device your child is using
  • No account is required
  • No email is collected
  • No cookies are set
  • No tracking pixels are used

COPPA Compliance

  • ThinkMate is designed for children ages 8-12
  • No personal information is collected from children
  • All data stays on the device being used
  • No social features — no chat with other users
  • No external links within the tutoring interface
  • Content is filtered to educational material only

Parent Dashboard

  • The parent dashboard is a local view of what your child has learned on that specific device
  • It requires a 4-digit PIN (set to 1234 by default, changeable by the parent)
  • The PIN is stored locally on the device
  • No parent name, email, or contact information is collected

Safety Features

  • ThinkMate only generates educational content
  • No social features
  • No chat with other users
  • No external links within the tutoring interface
  • Content is filtered to educational material only

Terms of Service
